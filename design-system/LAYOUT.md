# Design System — Layout & Composition

Version **2.0**. Pair with [Art Direction](../design/ART_DIRECTION.md).

## Spacing starters
4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96px. Use for consistent relationships, not to force equal space on all sections. A few coherent project-specific roles are permitted.

## Container and responsiveness
Default max-width 1280px; horizontal padding mobile 20px, tablet 32px, desktop 48px. These are start values, not content-independent mandates. Optional 12/8/4 grid and installed Tailwind breakpoint defaults. Verify at 390/768/1440px and around actual transitions. Ensure correct reading order.

## Composition
- Rank information and choose a focal point. A low-priority company introduction should not obscure the offering.
- Choose image/text relationships by **content role**: environmental context may suit a split Hero, product categories may suit editorial rows or a grid, and forms typically need clear controls.
- Vary proportions, density, section rhythm, crop and typography purposefully. **Equal cards and equal columns are not automatic defaults**; unequal layouts are not inherently “novel UX.”
- Use whitespace to establish focus rather than to maximize emptiness. Review large unused areas when core facts fall below the fold.
- Responsive transformations should preserve visual hierarchy and meaningful imagery.

## Cards and borders
Cards, dividers, shadows and radius are valid when they support grouping or coherent presentation. Do not encase every block merely to look designed. No mandatory global radius; adopt a consistent project/component treatment.

## Functional constraints
Familiar navigation/forms; readable inputs, error/focus states, usable contrast, realistic wrap and mobile touch targets.
